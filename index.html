MODULE 1: INTRO TO AI

This module introduces the foundations of artificial intelligence, exploring what AI is and how it has evolved over time. You’ll learn about the difference between narrow and general AI, and how machine learning fits into the bigger picture. By the end, you’ll understand the role AI plays in everyday life and why it matters for the future.

MODULE 2: UNDERSTANDING ALGORITHMS

Here we dive into the concept of algorithms, the step‑by‑step instructions that power AI systems. You’ll explore examples of algorithms in action, from search engines to recommendation systems. This module builds a strong foundation for recognizing how algorithms shape decisions and influence outcomes.

MODULE 3: NEURAL NETWORKS OVERVIEW

This module explains the basics of neural networks, the structures inspired by the human brain. You’ll see how layers of nodes process information and learn patterns from data. By the end, you’ll grasp why neural networks are central to modern AI breakthroughs like image recognition and natural language processing.

MODULE 4: DATA & TRAINING

Data is the fuel of AI, and this module shows how it’s collected, cleaned, and prepared. You’ll learn about training datasets, validation, and the importance of quality data. By the end, you’ll understand how data impacts accuracy and reliability in AI models.

MODULE 5: ETHICS IN AI

This module explores the ethical challenges of AI, including bias, fairness, and accountability. You’ll examine real‑world examples of ethical dilemmas and how they affect society. By the end, you’ll be able to think critically about responsible AI use and its long‑term impact.

MODULE 6: TESTING & EVALUATION

AI systems must be tested and evaluated to ensure they work as intended. This module covers performance metrics, error analysis, and continuous improvement. By the end, you’ll understand how testing safeguards reliability and builds trust in AI applications.
