# Product Requirement Document (PRD) – TSA Webmaster Competition

## Project Overview & Mission
- **Event**: TSA High School Webmaster Competition
- **Target Audience**: High School Students (Grades 9–12)
- **Theme**: Artificial Intelligence (AI) Learning Portal
- **Challenge Goal**: Demystify AI by providing foundational concepts, showcasing practical tools, and teaching ethical AI usage in academic settings.

---

## 1. Tech Stack & Architecture Constraints (Strict Compliance)
- **Allowed Tech**: Hand-coded HTML5, CSS3, Modern JavaScript (ES6+) or custom frameworks (React/Vue.js/Next.js).
- **Prohibited Tech**: No Wix, Squarespace, Webflow, WordPress, Joomla, or Drupal CMS platforms.
- **Compatibility**: Fully responsive across mobile, tablet, and desktop viewports. Compatible with Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Safari.
- **Accessibility**: Zero broken links, missing image alt attributes, or poor contrast color schemes.

---

## 2. Core Site Pages & Structure

### A. Navigation & Layout
- Fixed sticky navbar with smooth scrolling to sections and page links.
- High-contrast, sleek modern dark-mode aesthetic with vibrant accents (blue/purple gradients) tailored for high school students.
- Header links: `Home`, `Modules`, `Dashboard / Gamification`, `Interactive AI Chatbot`, `References`.

### B. Educational Content Modules (Minimum 3 Distinct Sections)
1. **Module 1: Fundamental AI Concepts**
   - Explanation of Machine Learning, Neural Networks, Large Language Models (LLMs), and Computer Vision.
2. **Module 2: Practical AI Tools & Techniques**
   - Prompt engineering guides, effective academic search tools, and study workflows.
3. **Module 3: Ethical AI Usage & Academic Integrity**
   - Plagiarism vs. assistance guidelines, bias awareness, copyright, and proper AI citation methods.

### C. Gamification & Progress Tracking Dashboard
- **Progress Tracking Bar**: Visual indicator showing % of completed learning modules.
- **XP / Points System**: Interactive quizzes at the end of each module that award Experience Points (XP).
- **Digital Badges**: Interactive badge grid unlocking achievements (e.g., "Prompt Master", "Ethics Scholar") upon completing modules.

### D. Interactive AI Chatbot Widget
- Floating action button in the bottom-right corner.
- Chat pop-up interface allowing users to test prompt techniques or ask quick questions about the learning portal.

### E. Mandatory Reference Page (`references.html`)
1. **Sources List**: Documented list of all research, image, and text sources.
2. **Framework & Tool Documentation**: Explicit declaration of HTML/CSS/JS frameworks used.
3. **Student Copyright Checklist**: Embedded or downloadable PDF checklist (1-page max).
4. **Resources & AI Reflection**: Embedded or downloadable PDF reflection form detailing GenAI usage, ethics, and project decisions (4-page max).

---

## 3. Rubric High-Priority Directives (For Max Points)
- **Design & Layout (20 pts)**: Exceptional user-friendly layout with intuitive navigation.
- **Graphics & Theme (20 pts)**: High-quality visuals, interactive elements, accessible color contrast.
- **Theme & Challenges (50 pts)**: Thorough coverage of high school AI education and academic ethics.
- **Function & Technicals (10 pts)**: 100% valid links, no broken image tags, full cross-browser compatibility.
- **Spelling & Grammar (10 pts)**: Flawless grammar across all modules and pages.
